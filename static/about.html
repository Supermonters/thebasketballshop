The relationship between Vietnam and the United States has grown stronger through the historic partnership between Nike and BasketballShop (Photo: Trí Dũng)

Our Mission

Our mission drives everything we do to expand human potential. We do this by creating breakthrough sports innovations, by making our products more sustainable, by building a creative and diverse global team, and by creating positive impact in the communities where we live and work.

Statement from the CEO & Founder of BasketballShop

"With a passion for basketball shoes and love of the sport, I initially just wanted to form a community of basketball shoe enthusiasts to share and trade with each other. But with the support of fellow members and encouragement from my beloved wife, I worked day and night to source authentic shoe inventory and earn the trust of our valued customers. The growth of Vietnam's largest basketball shoe retailer today is in large part thanks to your support."

Nguyễn Chí Dũng
CEO & Founder, BasketballShop — Top 15 Asian Entrepreneurs
